**Releases.** Tagging `v*` runs goreleaser via the shared `yasyf/homebrew-tap` workflow: bare `cc-review_<os>_<arch>` binaries, `checksums.txt`, a tar.gz archive, and the Homebrew cask. Do NOT hand-bump `plugin/.claude-plugin/plugin.json` in the release commit: CI asserts the manifest and the binrun descriptor (`plugin/bin/cc-review.binrun`) agree on version at every commit, and the descriptor can only move once the release's artifacts exist. Cut the CHANGELOG version, tag from a lockstep-clean main, and the release workflow's descriptor job syncs both files to the released version in its postrelease metadata commit.
